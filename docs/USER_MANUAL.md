## 🚀 Getting Started

### System Requirements
- **Browser**: Chrome 90+, Firefox 88+, Safari 14+, Edge 90+
- **Internet**: Stable internet connection
- **Screen Resolution**: 1024x768 minimum (1920x1080 recommended)

### First Time Setup
1. **Access the Platform**: Navigate to your Syncner URL
2. **Create Account**: Click "Register" and fill in your details
3. **Select Career Track**: Choose your learning path
4. **Complete Profile**: Add additional information
5. **Start Learning**: Begin your educational journey

### Login Process
1. Go to the login page
2. Enter your email and password
3. Click "Sign In"
4. You'll be redirected to your dashboard

## 🎓 Student Guide

### Dashboard Overview
Your student dashboard provides:
- **Progress Overview**: Current level and completion percentage
- **Learning Path**: Visual representation of your journey
- **Recent Activity**: Latest learning activities
- **Achievements**: Badges and milestones earned
- **Quick Actions**: Easy access to common tasks

### Learning Journey

#### 1. Understanding Levels
Each level contains three sections:
- **Learn**: Reading materials, videos, and tutorials
- **Test**: Quizzes and assessments
- **Exercise**: Hands-on projects and assignments

#### 2. Progress Through Learn Section
1. **Access Learning Materials**:
   - Click on a level from your dashboard
   - Select the "Learn" tab
   - Browse available materials

2. **Track Your Progress**:
   - Materials are marked as completed when accessed
   - Time spent is automatically tracked
   - Progress bar shows completion percentage

3. **Best Practices**:
   - Read materials thoroughly
   - Take notes for reference
   - Complete materials in order
   - Use external links for additional learning

#### 3. Taking Tests
1. **Test Access**:
   - Tests unlock after completing Learn section
   - Click "Take Test" when available
   - Read instructions carefully

2. **Test Process**:
   - Answer all questions
   - Review your answers before submitting
   - Submit when confident in your responses

3. **Test Results**:
   - Immediate feedback on completion
   - Score displayed with correct answers
   - Retake options available (if enabled)

4. **Retaking Tests**:
   - Check retake policy in test instructions
   - Previous attempts are recorded
   - Best score may be used for progression

#### 4. Completing Exercises
1. **Exercise Access**:
   - Exercises unlock after Test completion
   - Review requirements and resources
   - Plan your approach

2. **Submission Process**:
   - Complete the exercise as instructed
   - Prepare submission materials
   - Upload files or provide links
   - Add any required documentation

3. **Exercise Review**:
   - Staff will review your submission
   - Feedback provided within 48 hours
   - Resubmission allowed if needed

### Portfolio Management

#### 1. Viewing Your Portfolio
1. Navigate to "Portfolio" from the main menu
2. Your portfolio displays:
   - Personal information and achievements
   - Learning progress and skills
   - Certificate information
   - Recommended career roles

#### 2. Sharing Your Portfolio
1. Click "Share Portfolio" button
2. Choose sharing method:
   - Copy link to clipboard
   - Share via social media
   - Send via email
3. Portfolio is publicly accessible via the shared link

#### 3. Portfolio Features
- **Dynamic Content**: Updates automatically with your progress
- **Professional Layout**: Clean, employer-friendly design
- **Certificate ID**: Unique identifier for verification
- **Skills Showcase**: Displays learned technologies
- **Career Recommendations**: AI-suggested job roles

### AI Career Mentor

#### 1. Accessing AI Chat
1. Look for the floating AI chat button (bottom-right corner)
2. Click to open the chat interface
3. Start typing your question

#### 2. Using AI Chat Effectively
- **Ask Specific Questions**: "How do I create a React component?"
- **Request Code Examples**: "Show me a JavaScript function example"
- **Get Career Advice**: "What skills should I focus on for web development?"
- **Clarify Concepts**: "Explain the difference between SQL and NoSQL"

#### 3. AI Chat Features
- **Context-Aware**: Understands your learning progress
- **24/7 Availability**: Always ready to help
- **Learning-Focused**: Tailored to your career track
- **Safe Environment**: Monitored for appropriate content

### Attendance and Engagement

#### 1. Marking Attendance
1. Attendance is automatically tracked when you:
   - Log into the platform
   - Complete learning activities
   - Submit tests or exercises
2. Manual attendance marking available if needed

#### 2. Viewing Attendance
1. Go to your dashboard
2. Check the attendance widget
3. View:

   - Attendance percentage
   - Monthly calendar view

#### 3. Maintaining Streaks
- **Daily Engagement**: Log in and complete activities

- **Active Participation**: Submit assignments on time

### Leaderboard and Gamification

#### 1. Understanding Scores
Your leaderboard score is calculated based on:
- **Progress Score**: Completion of learning materials
- **Attendance Score**: Regular platform engagement
- **Consistency Score**: Steady learning pace
- **Time Score**: Efficient learning habits

#### 2. Viewing Rankings
1. Access leaderboard from dashboard
2. See your current rank
3. Compare with peers in your career track
4. View top performers

#### 3. Improving Your Rank
- **Complete Materials**: Finish all learning content

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

### Common Issues

#### 1. Login Problems
**Issue**: Cannot log in to the platform
**Solutions**:
- Verify email and password are correct
- Check for caps lock or typing errors
- Clear browser cache and cookies
- Try different browser or incognito mode
- Contact support if password reset doesn't work

#### 2. Progress Not Saving
**Issue**: Learning progress not being recorded
**Solutions**:
- Ensure stable internet connection
- Refresh the page and try again
- Check if browser allows cookies
- Verify you're logged in properly
- Contact support if issue persists

#### 3. File Upload Issues
**Issue**: Cannot upload files for exercises
**Solutions**:
- Check file size limits (usually 10MB)
- Verify file format is supported
- Ensure stable internet connection
- Try uploading one file at a time
- Contact support for large file uploads

#### 4. Test Submission Problems
**Issue**: Cannot submit test answers
**Solutions**:
- Ensure all questions are answered
- Check internet connection
- Refresh page and try again
- Verify test hasn't expired
- Contact support if submission fails

#### 5. Portfolio Not Updating
**Issue**: Portfolio shows outdated information
**Solutions**:
- Wait a few minutes for updates to process
- Refresh the portfolio page
- Check if profile information is complete
- Verify learning progress is being recorded
- Contact support if updates don't appear

### Browser-Specific Issues

#### Chrome
- Clear browsing data regularly
- Disable extensions that might interfere
- Update to latest version
- Check JavaScript is enabled

#### Firefox
- Clear cache and cookies
- Disable tracking protection temporarily
- Update to latest version
- Check pop-up blocker settings

#### Safari
- Clear website data
- Disable content blockers
- Update to latest version
- Check privacy settings

#### Edge
- Clear browsing data
- Disable tracking prevention
- Update to latest version
- Check security settings

### Mobile Device Issues

#### iOS (Safari)
- Update to latest iOS version
- Clear Safari data
- Check JavaScript settings
- Ensure stable internet connection

#### Android (Chrome)
- Update Chrome browser
- Clear app data
- Check internet permissions
- Restart device if needed

## ❓ FAQ

### General Questions

**Q: How do I reset my password?**
A: Click "Forgot Password" on the login page, enter your email, and follow the instructions in the reset email.

**Q: Can I change my career track after starting?**
A: Contact your administrator or support team to discuss career track changes.

**Q: Is there a mobile app available?**
A: The platform is fully responsive and works on mobile browsers. No separate app is required.

**Q: How often is my progress saved?**
A: Progress is saved automatically as you complete activities. Manual saves are not required.

### Learning Questions

**Q: Can I retake tests?**
A: Retake policies vary by test. Check the test instructions for specific retake rules.

**Q: How long do I have to complete exercises?**
A: Exercise deadlines are set by your institution. Check with your staff or admin for specific timelines.

**Q: What happens if I fail a test?**
A: Depending on the retake policy, you may be able to retake the test or may need to review materials and try again.

**Q: Can I access learning materials offline?**
A: Currently, all materials require an internet connection. Download features may be available for specific content.

### Technical Questions

**Q: What browsers are supported?**
A: Chrome 90+, Firefox 88+, Safari 14+, and Edge 90+ are fully supported.

**Q: Why is the platform slow?**
A: Check your internet connection, clear browser cache, or try a different browser. Contact support if issues persist.

**Q: Can I use the platform on multiple devices?**
A: Yes, you can access your account from any supported device with your login credentials.

**Q: How do I report a bug or issue?**
A: Contact your support team or use the feedback feature in the platform.

### Privacy and Security

**Q: Is my data secure?**
A: Yes, we use industry-standard security measures to protect your data and privacy.

**Q: Who can see my progress?**
A: Your progress is visible to you, your assigned staff, and administrators as needed for educational purposes.

**Q: Can I delete my account?**
A: Contact your administrator to discuss account deletion and data retention policies.

**Q: How is my portfolio data used?**
A: Portfolio data is used to showcase your learning achievements and may be shared with potential employers with your consent.
